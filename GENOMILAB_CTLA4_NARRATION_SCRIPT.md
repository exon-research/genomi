connected? The patient brings that question to GenomiLab.

## 00:06.6

## 00:42.0

Two repeats show reduced transendocytosis, but an apparently healthy mother
carries Q76H. That conflict matters.

## 00:49.0

views. CTLA4-pathway dysfunction leads, but alternatives remain open.

## 00:57.0

evidence-linked questions to take to their specialist.
